Something from my heart

Varsha, shayad main kabhi perfectly words mein explain na kar paun ki aap mere liye kya ho. 🥺🤍

Sach kahun toh mujhe aapse bahut zyada pyaar ho gaya hai. Itna ki aap meri life ka ek bahut important part ban gayi ho.

Aapse baat ho jaaye toh din thoda aur achha lagta hai, aur jab baat nahi hoti toh aapki yaad aati rehti hai. 🥺

Kabhi-kabhi khud se bhi poochta hoon ki 8 months tak baat na hone ke baad bhi main aapko bhool kyun nahi paaya... aur shayad iska jawab mere paas bhi nahi hai.

Main aapko kisi cheez ke liye force nahi karna chahta. Aapki feelings, aapki comfort aur aapke decisions mere liye important hain. 🤍

Bas chahta hoon ki aap kabhi ye na socho ki meri feelings sirf ek temporary attraction hain. Jo bhi hai, genuinely hai.

Pata nahi future kya hoga... lekin aaj jo feel karta hoon, woh dil se karta hoon.

Aap mere liye ordinary nahi ho, Varsha... aap genuinely bahut special ho. ❤️
